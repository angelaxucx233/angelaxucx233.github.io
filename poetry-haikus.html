Poetry

April 2026

Haikus

Morning

Steam wisps waft upwards. Sirens wail. Birds beep. Leaves flutter. Tea splashes downwards.

Remembrance

Snow dappled pine trees Open meadows, swimming pools, Hours on the roof.

Spring

Winter whorled by. World tilts. Sunlight settles in. Fungus gnats fly by.

Morning at Yale

School buzzing aloud. Sunlight dappled. All serene. Breathe out. Here am I.

By Angela Xu. Morning and Remembrance first published in 295 Magazine, Yale Asian American Cultural Center, 2026.
