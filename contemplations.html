Contemplations

The slower, inward work of thinking about what matters

Not arguments but attentiveness. These pieces sit closer to the diary than the essay — written in the aftermath of something heard in church, or during a long walk, or in the middle of the night when the mind will not be quiet. They are offered here not as conclusions but as companionship in the asking.
