卖油翁

The Old Oil Seller

Ouyang Xiu (欧阳修)  ·  Song Dynasty

陈康肃公善射，当世无双，公亦以此自矜。

Lord Chen Kangsu was skilled in archery — there was not another like him in his time. He congratulated himself because of this.

尝射于家圃，有卖油翁释担而立，睨之久而不去。见其发矢十中八九，但微颔之。

Once,1 while he was practising in his garden, an old oil seller set down his shoulder pole and stood up, glancing sideways at Lord Chen for a long while without leaving. When he saw that out of every ten arrows, eight or nine landed on target, he merely nodded slightly.

康肃问曰：“汝亦知射乎？吾射不亦精乎？”翁曰：“无他，但手熟尔。”

Kangsu asked him, “Do you also know how to shoot? Is not my shooting finely skilled?”

The old man replied, “There’s nothing to it — your hand is merely skilled.”

康肃忿然曰：“尔安敢轻吾射！”翁曰：“以我酌油知之。”

Kangsu became enraged and said, “How dare you slight my archery!”

The old man replied, “Because I know from pouring oil.”

乃取一葫芦置于地，以钱覆其口，徐以杓酌油沥之，自钱孔入，而钱不湿。

Then the old oil seller took a gourd and placed it on the ground. He covered its mouth with a coin and slowly used a ladle to pour oil in a thin stream into it. The oil entered through the hole in the coin, but the coin was not wet at all.

因曰：“我亦无他，惟手熟尔。”康肃笑而遣之。

Hence, he said, “I also have no special skill — only my hand is familiar with the task.”

Kangsu laughed and let the old man go.

  1. ↑ The original text does not include “once.”

Translation by Angela Xu. Original text by Ouyang Xiu (欧阳修, 1007–1072), Song Dynasty.
